# Maintenance guidance (generic project notes, replace with your own manuals and site procedures)

When a bearing fault is suspected, confirm the diagnosis with a second measurement before acting: repeat the recording
under a similar load and speed, and compare with the previous baseline. A falling health trend combined with a spectral
peak at the expected characteristic frequency is stronger evidence than either alone.

Typical inspection items: lubricant condition and quantity, shaft alignment, belt or coupling tension, mounting fit and
looseness, contamination and seal condition, and electrical causes such as bearing currents on inverter-driven motors.
Increase the monitoring frequency while the health index is falling. Plan replacement according to the site's
maintenance policy and the criticality of the machine. The system provides decision support; a qualified engineer
makes the final maintenance decision.
